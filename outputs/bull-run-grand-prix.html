DRIVER’S GUIDE / CONTROLS & POWER-UPS

Steer ← → or A D (curves push you wide) · Jump ↑ W Space · Slide ↓ S · Use slot 1 2 3 (or Z X C) · P pause
Phone: hold the left or right edge to steer, swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide, tap a slot to fire it.

Margin is your healthThe big bar above your weapons. Every hit takes a chunk. At zero you're margin-called and out of the race.
Three weapon slotsCarry up to 3 weapons. With all 3 slots full of different weapons, you can't pick up a new kind.
Stack to level upPick up a weapon you already hold and it levels up, up to LV3. Using a weapon spends the whole slot.
